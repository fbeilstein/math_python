\section*{Problem 1: The Initial Packet}

\subsubsection*{Mathematical part}
\textbf{Given:} A position spatial grid $x$, a centroid $x_0$, a width $\sigma$, and a momentum $k_0$. \\
\textbf{Derive:} The discrete normalization procedure. The continuous formula for the wave packet is given by:
$$
\psi(x) = \left(\frac{1}{2\pi \sigma^2}\right)^{1/4} \exp\left(-\frac{(x-x_0)^2}{4\sigma^2}\right) e^{i k_0 x}

Because we are working on a discrete grid, the continuous normalization constant is only an approximation. \\

\section*{Problem 4: Split-Operator}

\subsubsection*{Mathematical part}
\textbf{Given:} The full potential $V(x)$. The Trotter formula splits the propagator:
$$ e^{-i \hat{H} \Delta t } \approx e^{-i \hat{V} \Delta t/2 } \, e^{-i \hat{T} \Delta t } \, e^{-i \hat{V} \Delta t/2 } $$

\textit{Feynman Diagram Analogy:} This splitting is mathematically equivalent to the lowest-order term of a Dyson series expansion. The $e^{-i \hat{T} \Delta t}$ term is the \textbf{bare propagator} (the internal line representing free propagation through empty space), and the $e^{-i \hat{V} \Delta t / 2}$ terms are the \textbf{interaction vertices} (where the particle interacts with the external classical field and receives a momentum kick).

    \diagram* {
      (a) -- [fermion, edge label'=$\psi(t)$] (b) -- [fermion, edge label'=$e^{-i\hat{T}\Delta t}$] (c) -- [fermion, edge label'=$\psi(t+\Delta t)$] (d),
      (b) -- [photon, edge label=$e^{-i\hat{V}\Delta t/2}$] (v1),

\section*{Problem 7: The State Searcher Engine}

A stationary state is an energy eigenstate; its probability density does not change in time. We will build an engine that provides full thermodynamic control over an evolving wave, allowing us to interactively hunt for stationary states!

\subsubsection*{Mathematical part}
\textbf{Derive 1 (Expected Energy):} The continuous integral formula for the total expected energy $\langle E \rangle = \int \psi^* \hat{H} \psi dx$.

\textbf{Derive 2 (Heating / Kicking):} If we instantly multiply a wavefunction by a spatial phase gradient $\psi' = \psi e^{i p x}$, we inject momentum. Prove that the new expected energy is a quadratic equation:
$$E' = E + p \langle \hat{p} \rangle + \frac{1}{2} p^2$$
Solve this equation for $p$ to find the exact momentum kick required to reach a target energy $E_{target}$.

\textbf{Derive 3 (Cooling / Imaginary Time):} If we replace real time $t$ with imaginary time $\tau = -it$, the unitary operator $e^{-i\hat{H}t}$ becomes the decay operator $e^{-\hat{H}\tau}$. If the initial state is a superposition of eigenstates $\psi(0) = \sum c_n \phi_n$, prove that as $\tau \to \infty$, the state becomes entirely dominated by the fundamental ground state $\phi_0$ (assuming we continually re-normalize it to prevent it from vanishing).

\textbf{Derive 4 (State Searcher / Gram-Schmidt):} Prove that if we force the ground state coefficient to zero via orthogonal projection, $\psi \to \psi - \langle \phi_0 | \psi \rangle \phi_0$, the imaginary time evolution will instead cool perfectly into the first excited state $E_1$.
